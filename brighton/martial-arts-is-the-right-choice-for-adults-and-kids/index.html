Why Elements Martial Arts is the Right Choice for Adults and Kids

If you’ve been searching for martial arts in Brighton, you’ve probably noticed there are a handful of options. But not all martial arts clubs are the same, and at Elements Martial Arts Brighton, we’ve spent over a decade building something we’re genuinely proud of. Whether you’re an adult looking to get fit, learn self-defence, and take on a new challenge, or a parent searching for a structured, confidence-building activity for your child, this guide will tell you exactly what we offer, what sets us apart, and how to get started.

What Is Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) is a ground-based martial art that focuses on technique, leverage, and problem-solving rather than size or strength. It’s sometimes called “the gentle art” because a smaller, well-trained person can genuinely control and submit a much larger opponent using skill alone.

BJJ is one of the fastest-growing martial arts in the UK, and for good reason. It’s practical, it’s physical, and it’s one of the most effective forms of self-defence available. It also happens to be one of the best workouts you’ll ever do. At Elements Martial Arts Brighton, we teach both gi (traditional uniform) and no-gi BJJ, so there’s always something to suit your preference and your schedule.

Adult Classes in Brighton

Our adult programme is structured so that complete beginners and experienced practitioners can both thrive. We run three distinct tiers to ensure you train at the right pace:

1. Fundamentals

Perfect if you’ve never trained before. There is no fitness test and absolutely no experience required. Just show up, and our coaching team will take care of the rest.

2. Applied Fundamentals

Designed for those who have the basics down and are ready to start putting techniques together in live, controlled training scenarios.

3. Intermediate / Advanced

For experienced grapplers who want to sharpen their game, prepare for competitions, or simply train at a higher intensity. Whether you’re a 30-year-old professional looking for a rewarding new hobby, someone who wants to compete, or just trying to get out of the boring gym routine to do something that actually engages your brain , BJJ at Elements is worth trying.

Kids Martial Arts in Brighton

Finding the right youth activity in Brighton isn’t always easy. You want something that keeps them engaged, builds real-life skills, and puts them in a positive environment surrounded by inspiring role models.

Our kids BJJ programme ticks all of those boxes.

What Your Child Will Gain:

  • Unshakeable Confidence: Learning to handle themselves physically builds self-assurance that carries over into school and everyday life.
  • Discipline and Focus: Our classes are structured and purposeful, but delivered in a fun, games-based format that keeps children fully engaged.
  • Resilience: BJJ teaches children how to deal with difficulties, keep going when challenged, and learn from mistakes.
  • Real Anti-Bullying Skills: Practical self-defence awareness and boundary-setting taught in a safe, controlled environment.
  • A Sense of Belonging: Our kids classes have a strong community feel, and children genuinely look forward to coming back every week.

Our grading pathway gives children clear, tangible goals to work towards, and the progression from white belt upwards is a source of real pride for our students. We welcome children aged 5 and above.
