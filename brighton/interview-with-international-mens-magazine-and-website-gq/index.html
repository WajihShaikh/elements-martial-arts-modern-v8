Interview with International Men’s magazine and website GQ

05 Dec Interview with International Men’s magazine and website GQ

Posted at 11:35h in News by dvbiz

Head coach, Yousuf Nabi has recently had a published interview with International Men’s magazine and website GQ.

What is Jiu-Jitsu?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a form of martial art which sprung out of followers of judo and Japanese jiu-jitsu in Brazil in the early 1900s. According to Yousuf Nabi, owner of Elements Martial Arts gym in Brighton, the inventors of the sport adapted the pre-existing template to allow it to be used by smaller and weaker people to out-grapple larger opponents.

“It works based on getting your opponent onto the ground and then using positioning and leverage to pin them in a certain position,” Nabi explains. “You can use it as a form of self-defence, a competitive sport, or a fun and healthy hobby.”
