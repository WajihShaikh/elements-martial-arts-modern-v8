Reasons why your child should train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

13 Jul Reasons why your child should train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Posted at 11:17h in BJJ by yousuf

In this age where most kids will choose to just stay home and probably play video games or watch movies, the benefit of making them move out and learn something is paramount. Learning Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu will help your kid stay fit as well as make new friends instead of staying indoors and playing video games. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a perfect example of keeping fit, having fun and socializing. To help you get why Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is so important, here are the reasons why:

Your child will learn respect and discipline.

You have probably seen in movies how the masters of martial arts teach their students respects and discipline. Well, that’s what martial arts is all about. Martial arts teaches people of all ages both respect and discipline. These are attributes that usually requires training and attention not functions that are built in the mind or functions that are unchangeable. On the contrary disciple and respect are functions that are learnt through proper practice and guidance from an expert.

Respect and discipline extends far beyond the experience of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class. The instructor teaches your kid in the same way a teacher in a school setting will teach him. Your child needs to appreciate the fundamentals of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and the instructor will make sure he does so that he can enjoy learning. By appreciating and respecting the whole process, your child can develop the same respect for teachers as well as other authority figures. Any child who has respect and discipline will definitely go through the education system without problems and these attributes will also help him in future.

Confidence through self- defense.

Some people underrate the ability to defend yourself but honestly speaking, this is one of the most important skill one can ever have. When your child develop a clear understanding of all the techniques that he needs in order to defend himself, there is no doubt that he will become more confident.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu does not teach some complicated and unrealistic techniques unlike many other martial arts. What Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu teaches is something that is applicable in the real world and which can help your kid when the need be. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu usually balances the aspects of competition and self-defense (street) so you can be sure that your child is going to receive all the skills necessary for self- defense. This skill is very important to each and every child because in our current world you can never be sure about anything. Just because your kid knows how to punch and kick it does not mean he has the ideal skills for self- defense. There are many reasons why that is not ideal and one of them is the fact that striking in an uncontrolled situation can lead to unintended accidents. And even though it occurs as a result of self-defense, it might cause problems for you and your kid.

The perfect way of defending yourself is by trying to neutralize the situation and avoiding taking the damage and in such a case, no one will get hurt. If your child learns Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu he will be able to neutralize the situation by controlling the attacker and avoiding being hit. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu teaches one how to put the attacker on the ground and when he is on the ground he is under control. And if the attacker is stronger than him, he is taught how to avoid being pinned by him. When a child has this skills of self- defense, he will be feeling so much better about himself and hence the confidence. You should not be scared that your child will use the skills in an inappropriate manner because they are normally taught to use the skills only when they are really required. The idea behind Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu self-defense is defending yourself without having to use violence.

Your child will develop friendships as well as socialize.

Learning Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu will help your kid both physically and mentally as he is going to meet a lot of new people in class. From the other trainees, your kid can be able to make new friends. socializing is very important especially for children and taking your child in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training center will make sure he socialize enough just like he is supposed to.

Your child will be training with other kids sometimes older than him and this will help him develop the necessary social skills that will help him throughout their education and also in other aspects of life. Instead of leaving your kid alone in the house playing video games which cannot help him improve social skills, take him to a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class so that he can enjoy physical exercise as well as socialize with friends.

Improves your kid’s body awareness and coordination.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu has proven to be an excellent way developing motor skills in children. When your child learns Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, he is taught individual movements that are tied together through multiple sequences. Sometimes it will require some advanced movements and flexibility. Your child will learn these skills and movements which will help him even transform the same even in other sporting activities. Advanced body movement and flexibility learnt from Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu ensures that your child enjoy success in most of the sports that he participates in.

The excitement of a positive hobby.

At the end of the day, the happiness and positivity of learning something new is going to give your child total satisfaction. Everyone loves learning something new and your child is no different. Once you tell him about it, he will definitely be super excited. He will be counting days and looking forward to learning and meeting new friends at a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class. Don’t hesitate to take your child to a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu class because he is looking forward to it. Sign up today!
