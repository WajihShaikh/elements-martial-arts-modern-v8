Women’s-Only Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) Fundamentals Class

Join our Women’s-Only Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) Fundamentals Class.

This class is designed to provide a comfortable, welcoming, and supportive environment exclusively for women. Whether you’re a complete beginner or looking to strengthen your foundations, you’ll develop practical self-defence skills, improve fitness, and build confidence, all while training in a positive, empowering atmosphere.

The sessions focus on technique, movement, and application, making them the perfect introduction to martial arts for women who want to explore BJJ in a safe and encouraging space.

Our aim is to bring women together, fostering a strong sense of community where you can learn, grow, and thrive alongside like-minded training partners.

Your training journey

Are you new to BJJ?

When you first walk in our academy you notice that although everyone is training hard, they are also friendly and welcoming, and that the venue is clean and comfortable. We hope you take part on your first visit or stay to watch a class, using the opportunity to talk to an instructor regarding your training needs.

We have several Fundamental Classes perfect for you if you have never trained in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu. If you cannot drop into the Elements Martial Arts Academy, just call or e-mail us to discuss your training requirements. We’re committed to help you decide if Brazilian Jiu Jitsu is for you.
