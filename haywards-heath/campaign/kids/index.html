Meet the Coaches

At Elements Martial Arts, our coaching team brings decades of experience in martial arts. Through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, we help kids develop confidence, discipline, and essential life skills. All our coaches are DBS-checked, fully insured, trained in first aid, and certified in child safeguarding for sports.

Head Kids Coach - Nic Cartwright

Expert in Kids’ Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu | Over a Decade of Experience

Coach Nic has been teaching kids Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu since 2010, leading programs worldwide. Most notably, he ran a kids’ BJJ program on a U.S. military base in Okinawa, Japan, where he trained children from military families, helping them build resilience, confidence, and strong martial arts foundations. Now, as the permanent head coach at Elements BJJ, Nic is dedicated to creating a structured, fun, and empowering training environment for all young students.
