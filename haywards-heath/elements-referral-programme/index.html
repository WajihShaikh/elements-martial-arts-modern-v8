Elements Referral Programme / Referral Rewards: Earn £20 Each Time a Friend Joins

Love training at Elements?

Turn that into a proper win for your friends and for you. Join our Referral Rewards programme, and we’ll email you a unique discount code that you can share.

With your code, your friend gets to train for FREE for a whole week at Elements , a little treat from you (and from us) to help them start strong. (Without a code, the Free Week Trial is £20.) When they join any membership (adult or child, any contract length) and their first payment goes through, you earn £20.

  • Offer is valid for new members only

How it works (simple steps)

Step 1

Sign up using the form below

Once you sign up, we’ll email you personally a unique discount code (yours only), within 48 hours.

Step 2

Share your offer link + your code

Share the Free Week Trial page link with your friends and include your discount code.

Step 3

Your friend activates the offer at checkout

To activate the Free Week Trial (otherwise it’s £20), your friend must use your discount code at checkout in Glofox.

Step 4

Get rewarded

When your friend joins any membership (adult or child, any contract length) and their first payment goes through, you receive £20 by direct bank transfer (via Glofox)..

Inside Elements

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my discount code?

After you sign up, we’ll email you a unique discount code to share.

How does my friend activate the Free Week Trial?

They must visit the week trial page, follow the process and use your discount code at checkout in Glofox. Without a valid code, the Free Week Trial is £20.

When do I get paid?

When they join any membership (adult or child, any contract length) and their first payment goes through, you’ll receive £20 by direct bank transfer via Glofox.

Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?

No, refer as many as you like, earn £20 each time.

Can I refer someone who used to train at Elements?

No, referrals are valid for new members only.

Which locations does this apply to?

Brighton or Haywards Heath.
