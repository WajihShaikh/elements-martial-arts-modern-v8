Welcome to Elements Martial Arts Haywards Heath, where we specialize in providing tailored Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) programs designed to enhance your corporate workforce’s self-defence skills and team dynamics.

02 / Inside Elements

Empower Your Team with Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, renowned as the “gentle art,” emphasizes technique, strategy, and teamwork over sheer strength. This martial art equips individuals with the ability to effectively neutralize opponents through intelligent ground-based tactics. Our dedicated instructors will guide your team through the fundamentals, teaching them how to control situations and find solutions using locks and holds. Whether your employees are newcomers to martial arts or seasoned practitioners, our carefully crafted curriculum will ensure they develop a solid foundation in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

Enhance Collaborative Skills with MMA Training

Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) combines a diverse range of combat disciplines, fostering a comprehensive skill set that is highly applicable in real-world scenarios. Our corporate MMA program is thoughtfully designed to enhance striking precision, grappling proficiency, and overall situational awareness. From honing striking techniques to mastering takedowns and ground control, our experienced coaches will nurture your team’s growth. Our MMA training doesn’t just improve physical prowess; it cultivates mental resilience and the ability to adapt under pressure - crucial attributes for both self-defence and professional development.
