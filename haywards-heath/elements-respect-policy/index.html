Elements RESPECT Policy

Safeguarding Elements Martial Arts

Our Commitment

At Elements Martial Arts, the safety and well-being of our members is our highest priority.

We are committed to providing a safe, respectful and professional training environment for children, young people and adults. Any behaviour that compromises safety, welfare or personal boundaries will not be tolerated.

All safeguarding concerns are taken seriously and handled appropriately.

Club Safeguarding Officer

Elements Martial Arts has an appointed trained Safeguarding Officer:

  • Nusrat Nabi: nusratnabi1932@gmail.com

Nusrat has completed accredited safeguarding training and is responsible for receiving and responding to safeguarding concerns within the academy.

UKBJJA Safeguarding Oversight

As a UKBJJA-affiliated academy, we operate under the UKBJJA Safeguarding Framework.

All coaches at Elements Martial Arts:

  • Are registered with UKBJJA
  • Hold Enhanced DBS checks (as required by UKBJJA)
  • Have completed accredited safeguarding training
  • Hold recognised Emergency First Aid or Sports First Aid qualifications

Concerns may also be reported directly to the UKBJJA National Safeguarding Officer: safeguarding@ukbjja.org

Reporting

Concerns can be raised with:

  • A coach
  • Our Club Safeguarding Officer
  • The UKBJJA Safeguarding Officer

Serious or criminal matters should also be reported to the police.

Safeguarding is everyone’s responsibility. If something doesn’t feel right, speak up.

More UKBJJA Resources:

  • Safeguarding Procedures
  • Safeguarding Adults
  • Safeguarding Children Policy

As a gym, we continue to look out for each other and maintain a positive and welcoming atmosphere for everyone.
